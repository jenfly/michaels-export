Michaels order export

Download your Michaels Canada order history as a spreadsheet (CSV). Unofficial tool, not made by Michaels.

1. Install (one time, on a computer)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl + Shift + B (on a Mac, ⌘ + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button up onto your bookmarks bar:
    Export Michaels orders Drag it, don't click it.
Dragging doesn't work?
  1. Copy the code below.
  2. Right-click your bookmarks bar and choose Add page… (or Add bookmark).
  3. Name it Export Michaels orders, and paste the code into the URL box.

2. Use it

  1. Log in at canada.michaels.com and open your order history.
  2. Click Export Michaels orders on your bookmarks bar. A small panel appears in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose All orders or Date range, then click Download CSV.
  4. Wait for the progress bar to finish, and keep the tab open until it does. The more orders you export, the longer it takes: a month of orders is quick, but All orders can take several minutes. The file goes to your Downloads folder, and opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

The tool runs only in your own browser, using your existing Michaels login. Your password and order details are never sent anywhere else.
